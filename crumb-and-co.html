Cakes to order

Birthday coming up? Let’s bake.

A classic cake for 6–8 people starts at €38. Fancier ones cost a bit more. Mila and Sanne make every one by hand.

  1. Email us with the date, what you’d like and how many people.
  2. Give us 3 days. We order the ingredients and plan the baking, so tomorrow is too soon.
  3. Pick it up at the shop and pay then, cash or card.

Hello, we’re Crumb & Co

Baked with flour on our phones.

Sanne started Crumb & Co in 2019 in Utrecht. We bake sourdough, pastries and cakes, and everything is fresh from the oven, every morning.

Families from the neighbourhood, students and early commuters all end up at our counter. Welcome to the club.
